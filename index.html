Dear Jojo,

I honestly don't even know where to start, because sometimes words are not enough to describe how much a person can mean to you.

You are one of those people who make life a little more beautiful without even trying. Your presence, your random conversations, your silly jokes, your laughter, and even the smallest things you do can turn an ordinary day into a better one.

Maybe I don't always say it, and maybe I don't always know how to express it, but I'm genuinely grateful that our paths crossed. I'm grateful for every conversation, every laugh, every little moment, and even the random things that might seem unimportant to anyone else.

I hope you never underestimate how special you are. You deserve people who appreciate you, understand you, respect your feelings, and remind you of your worth.

I hope this new chapter brings you everything you've been wishing for. I hope you achieve your dreams, discover new places, meet beautiful people, and experience moments that make you genuinely happy.

One thing I really wish for is that we keep collecting memories, keep laughing at the most random things, and keep having those conversations that start with nothing and somehow last forever.

I don't know what the future holds, but I genuinely hope it keeps a place for our friendship. I hope we never become strangers after sharing so many little moments.

Thank you for being you, Jojo. Thank you for the laughs, the memories, and all the moments you probably don't even realize I appreciate.

You are not just another person I happened to meet. You are someone I'm genuinely glad became part of my story.

Happy Birthday, Jojo. ♡

With lots of care,
Adam
